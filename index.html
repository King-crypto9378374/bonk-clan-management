THE PEOPLE BEHIND BCMS

Founders

BCMS was created by VantaFR and Spaxx with a shared goal: help bring the Bonk.io clan community back together.

FOUNDER
VantaFR

Co-founder of BCMS, helping build a central place for clans to reconnect, organize, and compete.

FOUNDER
Spaxx

Co-founder of BCMS, working toward a stronger and more connected future for the Bonk.io clan scene.

Over time, the Bonk.io clan scene lost much of the activity and connection that once brought players together. Clans became less active, communities faded, and it became harder to find one place to organize. We created BCMS to help rebuild those connections—not by trying to recreate the past, but by giving the community tools to create its next chapter.

IN MEMORY OF KINGDOMLEGEND

A legacy behind the project

KingdomLegend was more than a Bonk.io name. He was a player who worked his way forward through persistence, learned from others, and eventually dreamed of changing the clan community for the better.

The beginning

His journey did not begin at the top. He started out struggling with the game and was nearly not accepted by some of the top players. He kept going and continued working to improve.

Learning from Hyperion

After meeting Hyperion, he received guidance that helped him develop his skills and master more of what the game demanded.

Retirement—and a bigger idea

Even after stepping away from competitive play, his connection to the community remained. He wanted to create something that could change Bonk.io and bring its clan scene together again.

The discovery of BCMS

The day after his passing, his brother found this idea among the folders on his drive. This project is being carried forward in memory of KingdomLegend and the vision he left behind.

Special thanks to Kuno, Tidaly, Hello4409, El_guero, and especially JC—his number one best friend. Thank you for being part of his story and his journey.
