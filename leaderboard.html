Leaderboard rules

  • Official stats are edited only by BCMS administrators and moderators in the Administration Panel.
  • Clan leaders can submit player stats for review; submissions do not change the public leaderboard until approved.
  • Players are ranked by ELO, highest first. Win percentage is wins divided by recorded matches.
  • Only stats from recorded matches should be submitted. False or unverifiable stats may be rejected.
